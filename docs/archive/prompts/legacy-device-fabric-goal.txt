- Each device runs a small unelevated agent with a deny-by-default capability manifest and device-local policy.
- Every job is target-bound, capability-bound, short-lived, nonce-bearing, replay-resistant, cancellable, and auditable.
- High-impact actions require a concrete preview and visible device-local consent.
- AI may propose a job but cannot mint rights, bypass policy, supply credentials, or silently broaden scope.
- Tailscale remains a separately installed private-mesh dependency. Mesh reachability is not job authorization; do not open public ingress or weaken ACLs.
