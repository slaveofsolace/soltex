# SteelSeries GG clean-room reference note

Date checked: 2026-08-09

Disposition: **REFERENCE ONLY**

## Brief

- Function sought: learn general hierarchy and information-density lessons for Soltex audio and workspace interfaces.
- Source: the user's already-installed, already-running SteelSeries GG desktop application, showing its Sonar mixer.
- Publisher/canonical public reference: SteelSeries; <https://steelseries.com/gg/sonar>.
- Access class: user-authorized local visual observation. No acquisition was required.
- Rights basis: proprietary application; no reuse, adaptation, redistribution, trademark, preset, DSP, or asset entitlement is asserted.

## Observed

- A narrow global product rail and a separate local Sonar tab row establish two levels of navigation.
- Channel identity is carried by restrained per-lane accent colors while the surrounding surfaces remain quiet and dark.
- Each visible lane gives one primary control dominant visual weight; preset, device, status, and routing information sit in smaller supporting rows.
- Secondary configuration is disclosed through compact tabs and settings controls instead of being placed into the global rail.
- The mixer can remain legible at high horizontal density because lane structure is repeated consistently.

## Inferred, not verified

- The repeated lane grammar may reduce re-learning as users move among game, chat, media, auxiliary, and microphone contexts.
- Separating global navigation, local mode selection, and per-lane controls may help Soltex keep dense tools from overwhelming the default workspace.
